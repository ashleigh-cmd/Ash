How you're employed

What is an EOR, and how does it affect you?

Untapped connects South African professionals with international employers. Because these companies don't have a South African legal entity, they use an Employer of Record (EOR) to employ you locally.

  • You receive a South African employment contract
  • PAYE, UIF and statutory leave are handled correctly
  • You're protected under South African labour law
  • Your day-to-day work is managed by your international employer, while the EOR handles payroll, contracts and compliance
You

Work day-to-day for your international employer, from South Africa.

Employer

Directs your work and goals, and is who you report to day-to-day.

EOR partner

Employs you locally: contract, PAYE, UIF and compliance.

Getting ready

How to prepare for your interview

Preparation makes the biggest difference. Research the company, understand the role, and be ready to explain how your experience matches their needs.

Prepare a strong introduction

Keep it to 1 to 2 minutes: who you are, your key achievements, and why you're interested in the opportunity.

Use real numbers

Demonstrate impact wherever you can, and be honest about career changes or gaps without speaking negatively about previous employers.

Set up for video

Test your technology, join early, choose a quiet, well-lit space, and keep a few notes nearby.

Address any gaps confidently

If you lack experience in one area, acknowledge it confidently and explain how you'd learn or adapt.

What to expect in your first interviews

The recruiter call and the client interview serve different purposes, so it helps to know what each one is actually for.

With your Untapped recruiter

A relaxed, two-way conversation to understand your fit before any client is involved.

  • Your background and experience
  • Your key achievements
  • Your career goals
  • Your motivation for moving roles

With the client

Typically two rounds, occasionally three. Your recruiter will always share more specific preparation tailored to that client beforehand, but here's a general overview.

  • First round: a 30-minute video call, similar in tone to your recruiter conversation but more numbers-driven, so it helps to have your billing and KPI figures ready
  • Second round: 45 minutes to an hour, usually with the hiring manager again and sometimes a senior team member too
  • The second round is more technical, so expect technical questions in written or verbal form

Questions worth asking them

Prepare thoughtful questions about success in the role, team expectations, and next steps. Remember, an interview is a two-way conversation.

Final checklist

  • Research completed
  • Achievements and metrics ready
  • Tech tested
  • Questions prepared
  • Arrive confident and authentic

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What happens after my first call?

Next is a short psychometric assessment covering English, typing, cognitive ability and numerical reasoning. It confirms your English fluency, since most of our clients are UK-based, and checks that remote work is genuinely the right fit for you.

Your recruiter then reviews the conversation and shortlists who to present to each client. If a client wants to meet you, you'll move into two client rounds: the first fairly standard, the second typically more technical. Your recruiter will brief you fully before each one.

Will I be employed by Untapped, the client, or the EOR partner?

Day-to-day, you work for the client. Your formal employment sits with our EOR partner, and Untapped manages the relationship throughout, so there's a single point of contact if anything comes up.

What hours will I be working?

Most roles are aligned to UK working hours, which in South Africa typically means finishing around 6 to 6.30pm.

How long does the process usually take?

It varies by client and role. Your recruiter will give you a realistic timeline once you're through the initial screening, and keep you updated at every stage.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your details are only shared with a client once we've spoken with you and confirmed you're interested in that specific opportunity.

What happens if I'm not right for this particular role?

We will always be honest with you. If it's not a fit, we'll explain why, and where relevant, keep you in mind for other roles that suit your experience.

What happens after I start?

Your recruiter checks in with you after you start, separately from the client relationship, so you always have someone to turn to.
